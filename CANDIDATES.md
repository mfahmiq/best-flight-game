# Candidate concepts — internal pairwise tournament

Judge criteria, in weight order:
1. Complete flight game shown in screenshots (cockpit/chase view, recognisable
   terrain, aircraft in frame, instruments lit, runway/target ring ahead,
   altitude/speed/heading on HUD).
2. Fun and depth — throttle and turn discipline rewarded, missions with
   different goals, fuel or damage turning a sortie into decisions.
3. Feel and polish — responsive controls at every speed, camera that never
   fights the pilot, landings and crashes that read as what happened.
4. Look and originality — terrain and sky in one style, an aircraft that
   looks built, instruments that look flown rather than drawn.
5. Structure — source split into files with one clear job each, named for
   what they do.

## The candidates

### A. Canyon Ring Racer
Pure speed: thread glowing rings through a red-rock canyon against the clock.
Fast to read, rings photograph well, but only one goal (time), no landings,
no fuel decisions, no runway — weak on criteria 1 (no runway) and 2.

### B. Island-Hopping Courier ("Skymail")
Bush pilot delivering parcels between island airstrips: take off, navigate,
land gently on short runways, refuel, next leg. Fuel, landing quality and
route choice are the decisions. Strong on 1 (runway + HUD + terrain) and 2,
but ring-free flying can look empty in a still screenshot.

### C. Mountain Rescue
Fly into high mountains, find a beacon, land on a short strip, pick up a
stranded hiker, fly home. Great drama and a clear story, but one long
mission, mountain terrain is hard to make readable at a glance, and a single
goal type is thinner on criterion 2.

### D. Aerobatic Ring Course
Stunt flying: loops, rolls and rings judged on style points. Fun to fly,
but "style points" are hard to show in screenshots, no runway, no fuel
story — weak on 1 and 2.

### E. Skymail Courier + Ring Run + Mountain Rescue (three sorties, one game)
One bush-plane game with three selectable missions sharing the same world:
(1) Ring Run — 8 glowing rings over the valley, timed; (2) Island Courier —
deliver to 3 airstrips, land and refuel each leg; (3) Mountain Rescue —
beacon in the high peaks, short-strip landing, pick up and return. Fuel,
damage, stall, gear and landing quality govern every sortie.

## Tournament (pairwise, criteria in weight order)

- E vs A: E shows runway + rings + HUD in its screenshots (criterion 1),
  and adds landings/fuel/missions (criterion 2). A is faster to read but
  thinner. **E wins.**
- E vs B: B is E's courier leg alone; E keeps everything B has and adds
  the ring course (screenshot-friendly targets) and the rescue story.
  **E wins.**
- E vs C: C's rescue is E's third sortie; E adds two more mission types
  and a valley runway that photographs better than a mountain strip alone.
  **E wins.**
- E vs D: D has no runway and no fuel story; E beats it on criteria 1
  and 2 outright. **E wins.**

## Champion: E — "Skymail — Bush Pilot Courier"

Three sorties, one world, one aircraft: a red-and-cream bush plane flown
in chase view over a seeded valley (fields, river, runway, mountain ridges).
Every sortie is governed by throttle discipline (fuel burn), turn discipline
(bank too hard and you bleed speed and stall), gear and flap decisions on
final, and landing quality (touchdown vertical speed and bank decide between
a clean landing, a bounce with damage, or a crash that reads as a crash).
The cover screenshot shows the plane chasing the next glowing ring with the
home runway ahead, lit instruments and full HUD — criterion 1 in one frame.
